          Help & FAQ

          How PineNotes works

          Guides for students and teachers.

          How do I download a note?

          Search or filter by department, tap Preview to check it, then Download ↓. The download is real — it saves the file to your device.

          How do I publish a note?

          Go to Publish, fill title + department, choose your file, and tap Publish. It is saved in your browser. For school-wide sharing, the school can approve a backend phase later.

          What file types can I upload?

          PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, MD, HTML, CSV, JSON. Keep files up to 50 MB for smooth browsing.

          How do favorites work?

          Tap ⭐ on any note. Favorite notes appear in the Favorites page and are stored only on your device.

          Can teachers moderate?

          In this static version, moderation is manual. The roadmap adds teacher approval — see README.md. Never upload harmful, private, or copyrighted material you don't own.

          Terms & Privacy

          PineNotes is free for learning. No payments, no ads, no account needed. Favorites and uploads stay in your browser (localStorage). Contact the team to report a note.

          🔒 Security — how PineNotes protects you

          • All titles/descriptions are escaped to prevent injection (XSS). • CSP header blocks external scripts. • Executable files (.exe/.bat/.sh/.js etc.) are blocked on upload. • Downloads are saved as files, never auto-run. • No tracking, no accounts. • Use Clear my data anytime to wipe your browser data. On a shared computer, clear data when you finish.

          ⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts

          / focus search · Esc close popups · Tab to navigate. Works with screen readers (skip link, labels).

          📶 Offline / Install as app

          PineNotes is a PWA — on Chrome/Edge you can “Install app”. Pages you visited work offline. No server or payment needed.
